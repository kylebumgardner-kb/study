Questions

When do I need to book?

By Thursday night for that Saturday's game. Earlier is better for Homecoming and Parents' Weekend — we only have twelve kits.

Do I have to be there for setup?

No. That is the whole point. Tell us your spot and we have it standing before you arrive.

What if it rains?

The canopy handles normal rain. If the game is cancelled outright, you get a full refund or we move your booking to another home game.

Can we book the whole season?

Yes — Greek chapters and clubs can reserve a kit for all five home games at a discount. Mention it in the booking notes.

Is food included?

Not in Basic or Terrier — you bring the food, we bring everything else. The Black & Gold package includes catering coordination.

What happens if something breaks?

Normal wear is on us. We only charge for gear that comes back damaged beyond use, and we will always tell you before charging anything.
